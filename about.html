My Philosophy

A photographer who values mood, connection, and calm

I’m Diana — a Newburgh-based photographer with 8 years of experience in weddings, engagements, and portraits. I approach every session with a gentle eye for detail, soft natural light, and a cinematic sense of timing.

My best work comes from relaxed, intentional moments. I want clients to feel confident in front of the camera, to enjoy the experience, and to receive a gallery that feels like an heirloom.
