Meet Diana

Photographs that bring you right back to a moment

Hi, I’m Diana Obermeyer! I’m a mom of two, a photographer of 6 years, and someone who believes the best photographs are the ones that bring you right back to a moment.

I love photographing all of life’s seasons—from growing families and playful little ones to engagements, weddings, senior portraits, and professional headshots. Whether I’m capturing a once-in-a-lifetime wedding day or the everyday joy of your family, my goal is always to create images that feel genuine, timeless, and true to you.

One of my favorite parts of photography is getting to know the people behind the camera. I love building connections with my clients and creating a comfortable environment where you can relax, have fun, and simply be yourselves. I want your session to feel less like a photoshoot and more like an experience you’ll genuinely enjoy.

When I’m not behind my camera, I’m usually spending time with my two kids, enjoying the outdoors, or working on our little homesteading life. I’m drawn to the simple, meaningful things in life—and I think that’s what makes photography so special to me.

Life moves quickly. My job is to help you slow it down, capture it, and give you something beautiful to hold onto.
