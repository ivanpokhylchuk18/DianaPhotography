About Diana

A photographer
with a poet's eye

I'm Diana — a Newburgh-based photographer specializing in weddings, engagements, and portraits. My work is guided by a single belief: the most powerful images live in the quiet, unscripted moments between poses.

With 6 years of experience and a film-inspired aesthetic, I craft galleries that feel like chapters from a beautiful story — warm, soft, and entirely yours.
